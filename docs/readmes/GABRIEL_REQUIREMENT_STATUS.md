## External decisions and acceptance dependencies

The [PRD decision register](PRD_GABRIEL_DEFENDER.md#15-decisions-risks-and-ownership) and [minimum decision inputs](GABRIEL_ACCEPTANCE_DECISIONS.md) require the actual pilot organization/console workflows and authorized lab access, browser/OS matrix, staging/production targets, approved model routes/residency/spend, analyst-adjudicated corpus and escalation destination. R3 also requires the selected response target, approvers and recovery authority. Generic lab implementation can proceed; real-console/model/response and human release acceptance cannot be invented from fixtures. User-authorized development does not supply those missing accounts, adjudications or customer operating decisions.
