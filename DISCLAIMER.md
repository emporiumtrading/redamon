# Gabriel operational notice

Gabriel — The Defender is a defensive analyst workbench. Use it only with systems, accounts and evidence you are authorized to access.

Browser observations are partial and source claims may be incomplete or wrong. Candidate findings, local rules, model output and stored knowledge do not by themselves establish an incident or prove security-control effectiveness. Review the cited evidence and coverage limitations before making operational decisions. Compliance coverage and security effectiveness remain separate questions.

Gabriel's current Docker path is a private, operator-led installation. It does not establish acceptance for public hosting, uninterrupted monitoring or autonomous response. Model routes and external actions require their own admission; adding credentials does not grant that authority.

Protect operator credentials, browser tickets, evidence and encryption keys. Follow the installation, backup, recovery and security-reporting documentation. Native Mac UAT and remaining release acceptance are recorded explicitly rather than assumed from a source merge.
